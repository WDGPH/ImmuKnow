Why the bars overlap

As soon as an original PDF is ready, another worker can make and check its protected copy. Encryption largely fits inside the time spent creating notices. Adding those two bars together would count the same seconds twice.

What takes time inside these jobs?

Most time goes into fitting each person's information onto the pages. The Typst engine stays warm between notices, reusing fonts and earlier work where it can.
